Word Completion and Sequence Prediction in Bangla Language Using Trie and a Hybrid Approach of Sequential LSTM and N-gram

Soumik Sarker, Md. Ekramul Islam, Jillur Rahman Saurav, Md Mahadi Hasan Nahid
IEEE · ICAICT 2020 2nd International Conference on Advanced Information and Communication Technology (ICAICT) (Published: Feb 2021)
Bangla NLP Sequence Prediction Trie Architecture Sequential LSTM N-gram Models Encoder-Decoder
IEEE Paper Presentation

Text composition efficiency in low-resource and morphologically rich scripts such as Bangla often suffers from cumbersome keyboard navigation. This paper introduces a hybrid sequence prediction and word completion pipeline fusing hierarchical Trie search structures with sequential Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) recurrent networks and N-gram statistical models. Evaluated across large-scale Bangla text corpora, the hybrid system demonstrates significant improvements in keystroke savings and prediction accuracy compared to standalone statistical or neural baselines.

A Survey on Blockchain & Cloud Integration

Soumik Sarker, Arnob Kumar Saha, Md Sadek Ferdous
IEEE · ICECE 2020 11th International Conference on Electrical and Computer Engineering (ICECE) (Published: Mar 2021)
Blockchain Cloud Computing Blockchain-as-a-Service (BaaS) Decentralized Security Access Control
IEEE Paper Presentation

Cloud computing provides on-demand computational scalability but raises centralized vulnerabilities in data integrity, auditing, and tenant trust. Conversely, decentralized blockchain networks enforce tamper-evident consensus at the cost of processing throughput and storage capacity. This survey systematically reviews integration architectures uniting blockchain and cloud computing paradigms, analyzing Blockchain-as-a-Service (BaaS) delivery models, consensus offloading mechanisms, privacy preservation schemes, and open challenges.

Deep Learning Approach Combining Lightweight CNN Architecture with Transfer Learning: An Automatic Approach for the Detection and Recognition of Bangladeshi Banknotes

Ali Hasan Md. Linkon, Md. Mahir Labib, Faisal Haque Bappy, Soumik Sarker, Marium-E Jannat, Md Saiful Islam
IEEE · ICECE 2020 11th International Conference on Electrical and Computer Engineering (ICECE) (Published: Mar 2021)
Banknote Recognition Lightweight CNN Transfer Learning Computer Vision Edge Inference
IEEE Paper

Automated currency recognition for individuals with visual impairments requires high classification precision paired with low computational latency suitable for edge and mobile environments. This study develops a lightweight convolutional neural network leveraging transfer learning and optimized feature extraction layers to detect and recognize Bangladeshi currency notes under varied lighting, angles, and physical wear conditions.
